# Sensor reference

Power Insight creates sensors on **one Home Assistant device per thing you
set up**: one for the grid connection, one per PV system, battery and
consumer, plus a **Combined** device for the whole home. The full list for
each lives on its device page:

Which sensors appear depends on what you turn on in the integration's
**Options**. Every sensor's card says which option creates it. *Total costs +
Levelized* means the **Total costs** toggle, with the cost method set to
*Levelized* or *Both*. See
[Sensor options & presets](sensor-options.md).

## Reading the sensor names

A handful of words come back in almost every sensor name. Once you know them,
the names explain themselves.

### Where power goes

The power-flow sensors on the grid, PV and battery describe **where that
device's power goes**, never what the device itself uses. They read
*Import to …*, *Production to …* or *Discharge to …*, followed by one of four
destinations:

| Destination | Means |
|---|---|
| **home consumption** | your household appliances, with or without their own sensor |
| **batteries** | charging a battery |
| **system standby** | the idle draw of your energy equipment, such as PV inverters at night. Not appliances on standby. |
| **grid** | export |

So a battery's *Discharge to system standby* is battery power feeding, say, a
PV inverter at night, not the battery's own standby draw.

### Ratio and share

Both are percentages, but they divide by different things:

- A **ratio** divides by **the device's own power**. A PV system's
  *Production to grid ratio* of 40 % means 40 % of *its* production is
  exported.
- A **share** divides by **a total for the whole home**. The same system's
  *Share of grid export* of 80 % means it supplies 80 % of *everything your
  home exports*.

### Rate and total

- A sensor ending in **rate** is **money per hour right now** (`EUR/h`),
  like a speedometer.
- A **Total** adds that rate up over time (`EUR`) and keeps counting across
  restarts, like an odometer. Totals work with Home Assistant's statistics
  and can be put behind a utility meter for daily or monthly figures.

### Standard and levelized

Money sensors come in two flavours, chosen in the options:

- **Standard** values electricity at your **live grid price**. Power from your
  own solar and battery counts as free.
- **Levelized** also counts what your **hardware** cost (purchase,
  installation, maintenance), spread over every kWh it delivers over its
  lifetime. It answers "is my solar or battery actually paying off?"

See [How it works](how-it-works.md) for the details.

### Savings, avoided cost and financial return

- **Avoided cost** is what your own power kept off your grid bill.
- **Cost savings** is avoided cost minus what the device costs to run.
- **Financial return** is cost savings plus what exporting earned.

## Units and currency

Power is in `W`, energy in `kWh`. The docs write money in `EUR` for brevity:
your sensors use **your Home Assistant currency** instead (`$/h`, `£`, …).
