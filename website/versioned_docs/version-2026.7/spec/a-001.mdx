## Why this case exists

Every other anchor case is a departure from this one. Nothing here is
restricted: each battery and consumer may draw from the whole mix, so each
sink's provenance row is simply the raw availability of the sources. If the
tiered logic in the solver is working, this is what it must collapse to when
there is nothing to tier.

It is also where the **home base load** is easiest to see. The metered devices
do not account for all the power entering the house; the remainder is drawn as a
dashed circle and competes for power exactly like a real device.
