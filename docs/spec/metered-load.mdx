## Why this case exists

Up to this rung the home base load *was* the house. Putting a meter on one
device splits it in two, and the interesting half is the one nobody measured:
the base load is not read from anything, it is what is left when the metered
draw is taken off the total.

Being derived is what makes it fragile, and the second snapshot is where it
breaks. `cons1` reports drawing 400 W while the sources between them supply
only 300 W. No arrangement of real electrons does that — it is two sensors
disagreeing, a scaling factor set wrong, or readings from different instants —
and the engine has to publish *something*. It clamps: the base load floors at
zero rather than going negative, and still publishes a share row, of zeros.

Whether a zero row is the right answer there, as against publishing nothing at
all the way an unavailable sensor does, is a fair thing to disagree with. It is
recorded here so the disagreement has somewhere to point.
