## Why this case exists

Nothing is added here. The same two devices are wired the same way, with one
configuration flag flipped, and that is the point — the whole export family
turns on a permission rather than on a device.

Reversing the grid changes its **kind**, not just its sign. An importing grid
is the largest source in the house; the same grid exporting is a sink, and the
diagram moves it to the other side accordingly. Everything downstream follows
from that one reclassification: the export channel takes a share of gross
power, the compensation is credited to whichever sources fed it, and
self-consumption is measured against what stayed home rather than against
everything produced.

The last snapshot is a guard rather than a scenario. The grid exports 500 W
while nothing at all is producing — a meter set that cannot physically happen,
but that a dropped sensor or a bad scaling factor will hand the engine anyway.
Gross power is exactly zero, and every ratio derived from it has to survive
being asked.
