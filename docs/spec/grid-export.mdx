## Why this case exists

Reversing the grid changes its *kind*, not just its sign. An exporting grid is
no longer a source at all — it becomes a **sink**, and a restricted one, because
only devices configured to export may feed it. Watch `bat2`: it is discharging
happily into the house, but it contributes exactly zero to the export, because
it may not.

That single reversal switches on three families at once, which is why this case
carries four snapshots rather than two: the export channel and its compensation,
standby draw being routed through the allocation like any other sink rather than
by gross share, and the pricing rule for a discharging battery — whose charge
mix happened in the past, where a snapshot engine cannot see it.

The last snapshot is a degenerate guard: nothing is producing and the grid is
exporting, so gross power is exactly zero and every ratio has to survive it.
