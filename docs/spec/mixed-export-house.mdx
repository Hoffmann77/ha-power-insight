## Why this case exists

Every device class at once, and nothing settled here for the first time. That
is deliberate: this rung is a net rather than a lesson. Each rule below it was
pinned in the smallest wiring that could express it, and this case asks whether
those rules still hold when they all apply to the same snapshot.

What it does add is **disagreement between devices of the same kind**. `bat1`
may feed the grid and `bat2` may not, and they are otherwise alike — both
discharging, both supplying the house. Watch `bat2` in the first snapshot: it
is contributing happily to consumption and contributes exactly zero to the
export, because it is not allowed to. A model that computed the export mix from
gross shares could not produce that.

The batteries also differ in what their storage costs, which is the only place
in the corpus where a levelized price is a genuine blend of two different
figures rather than a single source's number carried through.
